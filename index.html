Privacy Policy for py

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to py and PyDeca. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our desktop application, web interface, AI assistant, automation tools, and related services.

We aim to protect user privacy and explain how information may be collected, used, stored, and processed.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Messages, prompts, files, and other content you submit.
  • Application settings, preferences, and chat history.
  • Technical information used for troubleshooting or security, where enabled.
  • Information sent to AI providers or other connected services when you use them.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to provide application features, generate AI responses, maintain chat history, save preferences, troubleshoot problems, improve reliability, and protect the application.

4. Local Storage and AI Providers

Some information may be stored on your device. If you use a cloud AI provider or another connected service, relevant prompts, files, or other inputs may be transmitted to that provider for processing.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

5. API Keys and Security

If you configure an API key, its storage and handling depend on the application's implementation and configuration. Keep API keys private. If a key may have been exposed, revoke or replace it with your provider.

Do not assume that an API key is encrypted or securely stored unless the implementation explicitly provides that protection.

6. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with third-party services you choose to use, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the application and its users. We do not intend to sell personal information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain in local files, databases, backups, or third-party systems according to their respective configurations and retention policies. Removing local data may not delete information already sent to an external provider.

8. Data Security

Reasonable precautions should be taken to protect information. However, no application or method of electronic transmission can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children and Young Users

If you are not legally permitted to consent independently to the processing of your information, obtain any permission or supervision required by applicable law.

10. Third-Party Services

py and PyDeca may connect to external AI providers, hosting platforms, or websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not solely by this document.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The date at the top should be updated whenever appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer using the contact method published on the official py / PyDeca website or repository.

This policy is a general template. Before publishing it, verify that it accurately describes the application's actual data collection, storage, API-key handling, and AI providers.
