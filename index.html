This policy explains how py and PyDeca may access, process, and store information. The published policy must accurately reflect the actual features, permissions, and data practices of the version you use.

1. About py / PyDeca

py is a desktop application, and PyDeca is its web counterpart. They provide AI-assisted features, which may include chat, local AI processing, cloud AI integrations, and optional Google Drive access. Available features depend on the version and configuration.

2. Information the app may process

  • Prompts and content: Questions, messages, code, documents, and other content you choose to submit.
  • Google Drive data: If you connect Google Drive, the app may access account information, file metadata, or file contents permitted by the OAuth permissions you approve.
  • Settings: Preferences, selected AI provider, and configuration information required to provide app features.
  • Chat history: Chat history is intended to be stored locally on your device or in your browser. Other storage, such as logs or caches, depends on the actual version.

3. Google Drive and Google account data

If you choose to connect Google Drive, py / PyDeca uses Google OAuth to request the permissions shown on Google's consent screen. The app should request only the permissions necessary for the features you use.

Depending on the permissions granted and features implemented, the app may access selected files or file metadata to provide a requested Drive-related feature. Connecting Drive does not, by itself, mean that all files in your Drive should be read or transmitted.

If you choose to submit Drive content to a cloud AI feature, the selected content may be transmitted to that AI provider to generate the response you requested. Review any in-app disclosure before sending private or sensitive content.

Google user data must be accessed, used, stored, and shared in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements. Google account data is not sold or used for advertising by this app.

You can review or revoke third-party access in your Google Account permissions . Revoking authorization prevents future access through that authorization, but does not automatically delete information already transmitted to another provider.

4. Cloud AI providers

When you use a cloud AI feature, your prompt and any content you include may be sent to the selected provider to generate a response. Configured providers may include Google Gemini, Cloudflare-hosted AI services, or another cloud AI provider.

If a prompt includes text or a file obtained from Google Drive, that content may also be sent to the selected AI provider as part of your request. The app should not transmit Drive content automatically unless this behavior is clearly disclosed and authorized.

Third-party providers process information under their own terms, privacy notices, and service settings. Their data retention and processing practices may differ. Review their current policies before submitting confidential information.

5. Local AI processing

If a local AI model is available and selected, prompts may be processed on your device through that model. This does not mean every feature works offline. Features that depend on Google Drive or cloud AI providers require the relevant connection.

6. Storage and retention

Chat history is intended to be stored locally in browser or device storage, rather than in a central py / PyDeca chat-history service. The exact storage method may differ between versions.

Information sent to cloud AI providers leaves your device and may be retained by those providers according to their policies, account settings, and applicable service terms. Local deletion does not necessarily delete information already sent to a provider.

You can remove locally stored chat data using available app controls, browser settings, or device tools. Provider-side deletion requests, where available, must be made through the relevant provider.

7. Sharing of information

The app does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with Google when needed for authentication or Drive access, and with the selected AI provider when you initiate a cloud AI request. Other infrastructure providers may process information if required by the actual deployment. Any additional sharing must be accurately disclosed.

8. Security

No storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected and avoid sharing API keys. The app should protect OAuth tokens and API keys and must not expose secrets in public client-side code, public repositories, or inappropriate logs.

9. Children's privacy

Users should use the app only in accordance with applicable laws, age requirements, account rules, and the terms of Google and any selected AI provider. Where required, parent or guardian permission should be obtained.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Choose whether to connect Google Drive.
  • Choose whether to use supported cloud AI or local AI features.
  • Avoid submitting content you do not want sent to a cloud provider.
  • Delete locally stored data using available app or device controls.
  • Revoke Google authorization through your Google Account settings.

Privacy rights depend on your location and applicable law. You may contact the developer to ask about information handled by the app.
